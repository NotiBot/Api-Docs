Class APIGuildInfluencerPanel

Influencer Panel - members holding a role the admin chose add THEIR OWN stream from a panel posted in Discord, and Noti posts their lives, videos and posts with the server's settings.

The admin sets, per platform, the channel and ping role (and a second channel/role for videos or posts where the platform has them), the live role, a log channel, a "verified" role given while a member has an active stream, and how many accounts one member may add per platform. Influencer streams count toward the server's own per-platform limit; the per-member cap never raises it.

A member who loses every required role, or leaves, has their streams PAUSED (the creator is untracked and the slot freed); getting the role back resumes them while there is room. A stream that cannot come back stays paused with reason limit.

Tiers: free - one required role, cap 1. Premium - up to 3 required roles, cap up to 3, approvals, member text. Premium Plus - up to 10 required roles, the full panel editor, the Live Now board, webhook identity, cap up to 5. data.access from getGuildInfluencerPanel says what the server may set; a setting above it is refused with 403.

Behind the influencerPanel launch flag: every method answers 403 until it is beta/public for the caller, and 403 when the server's plan lacks the Influencer Panel.

updateGuildInfluencerPanel

  • updateGuildInfluencerPanel(__namedParameters): Promise<WebResponse<GuildInfluencerPanelUpdateData>>

  • Change the setup, field by field - omit one to leave it unchanged. platforms upserts the platforms listed and leaves the others alone.

    400 names the problem: a bad id, a platform switched on without a channel, a setting the platform does not have, or a channel/role Noti cannot use. 403 for a setting above the plan. Turning a Premium setting OFF is always accepted.

    After saving, every active influencer stream is updated to the new settings (and paused where its platform went off), a posted panel and board are redrawn, and switching the panel back on resumes what switching it off paused. applied, panelError and boardError report that.
