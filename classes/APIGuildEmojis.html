Class APIGuildEmojis

Reposition Emojis - reorder a server's emojis in Discord's emoji picker.

Discord has no reorder API: the picker lists a server's emojis oldest upload first. So Noti re-uploads the emojis that have to move, in the new order, and deletes the originals. Every re-uploaded emoji gets a new id: Noti updates its own settings (role panels, starboards, message buttons, <:name:id> in message text) and re-renders posted role panels, but emojis in old chat messages and other bots' settings may show as broken.

Only what must move is re-uploaded: emojis at the start of the new order that are already in age order keep their place. Integration emojis (managed) and greyed-out ones (available: false) can never move - pin them.

Free servers: one reposition per 7 days (nextFreeAt). Premium: unlimited (unlimited). Starting one needs a top.gg vote (403 vote_required, Premium skips it) and Manage Expressions for both the caller and Noti. Behind the repositionEmojis launch flag: every method answers 403 not_available until it is beta/public for the caller.
